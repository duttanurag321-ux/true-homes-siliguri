At the property

Walk the route you will use

  1. Start at the entrance. Save the location pin, inspect the last stretch of road and check how a second vehicle would pass. Try the drive at your usual travel time.
  2. Walk the boundaries. Ask someone to identify the exact plot or villa on the layout and on the ground. Record its reference beside your photos.
  3. Look at water and access. Check ground levels, visible drains, water supply and the approach after rain if possible. Ask who handles repairs.
  4. Stand in each room. Note daylight, privacy, ventilation and mobile signal. Distinguish display-home fittings from items included in the sale.
  5. Leave with a record. List your unanswered questions and ask for written answers tied to the same property reference.

Before a booking

Match the documents to the home

Request the seller’s or developer’s name, the plot or unit reference, the proposed agreement, the relevant permissions and the payment schedule. Make sure the documents refer to the property you saw.

For a registered project, ask for the registration number and look up the entry yourself in the West Bengal Real Estate Regulatory Authority’s project directory. Compare the project and promoter names. Open the WBRERA directory ↗

For farmhouse land, ask your own property lawyer to check title, permitted use, access rights and the proposed construction against the documents before paying a booking amount.

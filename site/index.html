Buying with True Homes

What to send your broker

At True Homes, a Siliguri property search can start with one WhatsApp message. Tell us where a normal week takes you and what you can spend in total.

Your shortlist
Give us two things you need and one you can compromise on. A weekday commute, ground-floor bedroom or space to garden is more useful than a long list of preferences.
Your figures
Tell us whether your budget includes registration, parking and finishing work. Ask for a dated, itemised quote for the specific home you’re considering.
Your visit
Share a preferred day and the place you’ll travel from. Ask for the entrance pin and confirmation of what you can inspect on that date.

Siliguri property enquiries

Questions buyers ask

Can I get the current price and availability?

Send the project name and your preferred home or plot size. Ask for a dated quote identifying the unit, the amount due at booking and the exclusions. Availability can change between a visit and a booking.

Can I visit both areas on the same day?

Send your starting point, preferred date and available time. Ask for both entrance pins and viewing confirmations before planning the route.

What should I ask about a farmhouse?

Ask what land and structures the sale covers, what uses the documents permit, how water is supplied, and who maintains shared access. Take the written answers to your own adviser.

Can I start my search from outside Siliguri?

Yes. Send your intended use and budget on WhatsApp. Ask for a location pin, dated site visuals and a written cost sheet before making travel plans.
